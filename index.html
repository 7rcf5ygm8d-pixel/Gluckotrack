About GlucoTrack

A student prototype for keeping glucose readings organized and easier to review over time.

Educational use only

Problem & intended users

People who monitor blood glucose may find it difficult to keep measurements in one place and notice simple patterns across days. GlucoTrack demonstrates a compact diary and trend view.

Intended users: people who record glucose readings and want to organize information for conversations with their healthcare professional.

Key features

• Manual measurement entry
• History table and trend chart
• Simple descriptive statistics
• CSV import for exported readings
• Mobile-friendly navigation

Data & privacy

The included readings are fictional. This prototype does not connect to a hospital record, send data to a server, or implement a real Bluetooth connection. Browser storage may be limited when opened as a local file.

A real product would require secure storage, access controls, consent, data minimization, and privacy review.

Limitations & future work

Current version is not clinically validated and does not diagnose, predict complications, or recommend insulin or medication changes. Future work could include validated device integrations, secure backup, accessibility testing, and clinical review.

The illustrative 70–180 mg/dL band is not a personalized treatment target. Follow an individual's care plan and ask a healthcare professional about readings of concern.
